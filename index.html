Yes. The General Report and Personal Risk check are free forever — no account, no token, no catch. The Deep Report and Exit Door are optional 1 USDC supporter reports for people who want extra forensic detail; they help keep the free reports free. Nothing free will ever be moved behind a payment.

You pay 1 USDC on Solana with Phantom (or any Solana Pay wallet). We never hold your funds or keys — the payment goes straight to the Reality Check wallet and our server checks it on-chain before showing the report. What makes Reality Check different?

Most tools only show numbers. We explain what the concentration means for the small retail buyer — in plain language.

Public sources: on-chain holder data and public market data. Everything we show was already public — we just translate it.

No. On-chain facts and plain-language interpretation only. Always verify yourself and never invest more than you can afford to lose.
